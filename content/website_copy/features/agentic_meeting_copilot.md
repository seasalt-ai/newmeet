# **SeaMeet Agentic Copilot**

**Your intelligent email-based agent that transforms how you interact with meeting data through conversational follow-up.**

---

## **What is the SeaMeet Agentic Copilot?**

The SeaMeet Agentic Copilot is an innovative email-based AI agent designed to enhance your productivity by enabling interactive, conversational access to meeting data. After a meeting concludes and you receive the initial summary, you can engage in a back-and-forth email dialogue with the Copilot to request information, generate new content, and perform complex tasks related to that specific meeting.

---

## **What the Copilot Can Receive**

### **Direct Email Replies**
Simply reply to meeting summaries with your instructions and requests.
- Generate summaries with different tones
- Create specific document types
- Request detailed analysis

### **Email with Attachments**  
Send files and documents for the Copilot to work with.
- "Use the attached file to create a video presentation"
- Incorporate external documents into meeting analysis
- Process additional context materials

### **Multi-User Email Threads** *(Future Capability)*
Enable team collaboration through email threads.
- Multiple team members can participate
- Collaborative content creation
- Shared meeting insights

---

## **What the Copilot Can Respond With**

### **Rich Text Responses**
Generate summaries, SOWs, and reports in plain text or rich markdown format.

### **Follow-up Questions**
Ask clarifying questions as part of agentic planning workflows.
*Example: "Based on your request to build a business plan, here's my plan so far... Is it ok to proceed from here, or would you like to refine it before I proceed?"*

### **File Attachments**
Send transcripts, PDFs, slide decks, and other documents directly to your inbox.
- Meeting transcriptions
- Executive summaries in PDF format
- Presentation slides for stakeholders

### **Email Drafts**
Create email drafts with correct recipients.
- **Layman's version**: Generate email drafts for manual sending
- **API version**: Direct Gmail integration for automatic draft placement
