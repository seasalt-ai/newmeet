Frequently Asked Questions

What do you mean by "Meeting Hours: unlimited hours / license"?

The "unlimited hours" rule apply on meetings recorded by SeaMeet. For instance, if you use SeaMeet to record your Google Meet, Teams, or Zoom meetings, you can record as many hours as you want. The real upper bound is limited by how many meetings you participate in a month. However, this does not include batch file uploads. For instance, you cannot batch upload a thousand audio files to use SeaMeet to transcribe all of them.

How does the Free plan work?

The Free plan includes 6 hours of transcription for lifetime use, supports 1 concurrent meeting, allows 5 audio files lifetime (first 3 minutes viewable), and records up to 5 hours per meeting (first 30 minutes of transcription viewable).

What's included in the Pro plan?

The Pro plan ($9.99/month or $99/year) includes everything in Free, plus 20 hours of transcription per month (no rollover), 100 audio files per month, speaker identification, Google Docs export, customized summaries, and additional transcription at $1 per hour.

How does the Premium plan differ from Individual?

The Premium plan ($19.99/license/month or $199/license/year) includes everything in Pro, but with unlimited hours per month per license. It also includes enterprise features such as vocabulary boosting, executive insights, and API access.
