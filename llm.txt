# SeaMeet

> SeaMeet (https://seameet.ai) is a bot-free meeting recorder and AI note
> taker by Seasalt.ai. It records meetings, calls, and anything on screen
> directly on your device -- no bot joins the call -- then produces
> transcripts (23 languages), AI summaries, action items, and chapters.
> Three ways to run it: instant in-browser recording at
> https://app.seameet.ai (no account or install), a Chrome extension, and a
> Windows/macOS desktop app. Recordings sync across all three with Sync Pro.
> AI agents can operate the desktop recorder through its MCP server.
