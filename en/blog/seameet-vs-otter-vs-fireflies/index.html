The SeaMeet Difference

While all three of these tools are great options for automating your meeting notes, SeaMeet offers a few unique features that set it apart from the competition:

  • Ineffective Meeting Detection: Our industry-first feature helps you identify and fix unproductive meeting habits.
  • Advanced Language Support: We offer advanced support for recognizing English words in non-English contexts, making us the ideal choice for global teams.

Pricing

All three tools offer a free plan, as well as paid plans for individuals and teams. Here’s a quick overview of their pricing:

  • SeaMeet: Paid plans start at $15/user/month.
  • Otter.ai: Paid plans start at $8.33/user/month.
  • Fireflies.ai: Paid plans start at $10/user/month.

Which one is right for you?

The best AI meeting assistant for you will depend on your specific needs and budget. But if you’re looking for a tool that can help you not only automate your meeting notes but also improve your meeting effectiveness, then SeaMeet is the clear choice.
