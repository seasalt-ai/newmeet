Language & Appearance

Chapter 20: Language & Appearance

Introduction

SeaMeet is a global application used by people in dozens of countries, speaking many different languages, with varying preferences for how their software looks and feels. This chapter is all about making SeaMeet YOURS—customizing it to speak your language, match your aesthetic preferences, and fit seamlessly into your digital workspace.

Whether you want SeaMeet in your native tongue, prefer a dark interface that's easier on your eyes at night, or need to adjust how dates and times are displayed to match your local conventions, this chapter has you covered.

Chapter Objectives

After reading this chapter, you will be able to:

  • Change SeaMeet's display language (16 languages available)
  • Switch between light and dark themes
  • Configure date and time formats for your region
  • Set up time zone preferences
  • Customize the appearance of the Floater and interface elements
  • Understand regional differences in number formatting
  • Make SeaMeet accessible and comfortable for your needs

Section 1: Language Settings

SeaMeet supports 16 languages, making it accessible to users around the world. The interface, menus, settings, and help text can all be displayed in your preferred language.

Available Languages

SeaMeet currently supports:

  1. English (US and UK variants)
  2. Spanish (Español) - Spain and Latin American variants
  3. French (Français)
  4. German (Deutsch)
  5. Italian (Italiano)
  6. Portuguese (Português) - Brazil and Portugal variants
  7. Dutch (Nederlands)
  8. Russian (Русский)
  9. Chinese Simplified (简体中文)
  10. Chinese Traditional (繁體中文)
  11. Japanese (日本語)
  12. Korean (한국어)
  13. Arabic (العربية) - Right-to-left interface
  14. Hindi (हिन्दी)
  15. Turkish (Türkçe)
  16. Polish (Polski)

Changing the Display Language

Step-by-Step:

  1. Open Settings

    • Click the gear icon in SeaMeet's left sidebar

  2. Navigate to Language

    • Look for "Language" in the left sidebar
    • Or may be under "General" settings

  3. Select Your Language

    Display language: [English ▼]

Click to see dropdown:
• English
• Español
• Français
• Deutsch
• [etc...]

  4. Click Apply or Save

  5. Restart SeaMeet

    • Some versions apply immediately
    • Others require restart to fully load translations
    • Close SeaMeet completely and reopen

  6. Verify Change

    • All menus should now be in your selected language
    • If partially translated, may be a work-in-progress language

Language Variants

Some languages have regional variants:

English:

  • English (United States) - "color," "center"
  • English (United Kingdom) - "colour," "centre"

Spanish:

  • Spanish (Spain) - "ordenador"
  • Spanish (Latin America) - "computadora"

Portuguese:

  • Portuguese (Brazil) - "você"
  • Portuguese (Portugal) - "tu"

Chinese:

  • Simplified (简体中文) - Used in mainland China
  • Traditional (繁體中文) - Used in Taiwan, Hong Kong

Choose the variant that matches your region for the most natural experience.

What Gets Translated

Fully Translated:

  • All menu items and buttons
  • Settings labels and descriptions
  • Error messages and notifications
  • Help text and tooltips
  • The user manual (if available in that language)

Not Translated:

  • Your recording file names (use your own naming)
  • Content within recordings (obviously)
  • Third-party app names ("Zoom" stays "Zoom")
  • Technical logs and debug information

When Language Doesn't Change

If Language Stays in English:

  1. Apply and Restart:

    • Some versions need restart
    • Close SeaMeet completely
    • Reopen

  2. Check Language Pack:

    • Some languages require separate download
    • Check if there's a "Download Language Pack" button

  3. Verify Selection:

    • Reopen settings
    • Confirm your language is selected
    • Try selecting a different language, then back to yours

  4. Update SeaMeet:

    • Older versions may have limited language support
    • Check for updates

Section 2: Theme and Appearance

SeaMeet offers different visual themes to match your preferences and reduce eye strain.

Available Themes

Light Theme (Default):

☐ Light

Appearance:

  • White or light gray background
  • Dark text on light background
  • Bright, clean look
  • Traditional Windows/macOS appearance

Best For:

  • Daytime use
  • Well-lit environments
  • Users who prefer bright interfaces
  • Matching standard operating system theme

Light Theme Colors:

  • Background: #FFFFFF (white) or #F5F5F5 (light gray)
  • Text: #333333 (dark gray/black)
  • Accent: Your system accent color or SeaMeet blue
  • Sidebar: Light gray with dark icons

Dark Theme:

☐ Dark

Appearance:

  • Dark gray or black background
  • Light text on dark background
  • Easy on the eyes in low light
  • Modern, sleek look

Best For:

  • Nighttime use
  • Dark room environments
  • Reducing eye strain
  • Matching dark mode operating system
  • OLED displays (saves battery)

Dark Theme Colors:

  • Background: #1E1E1E (dark gray) or #000000 (black)
  • Text: #FFFFFF (white) or #E0E0E0 (light gray)
  • Accent: Brighter accent color for visibility
  • Sidebar: Dark with light icons

Benefits of Dark Mode:

  • Reduced eye strain in low light
  • Less blue light emission
  • Better battery life on OLED screens
  • Reduced glare

Auto/System Theme:

☐ Auto (Follow System)

Appearance:

  • Automatically matches your operating system theme
  • Light when Windows/macOS is in light mode
  • Dark when Windows/macOS is in dark mode

Best For:

  • Users who switch system themes frequently
  • Those who want SeaMeet to match everything else
  • Automatic adaptation to time of day (if OS supports)

Changing the Theme

Step-by-Step:

  1. Open Settings

    • Click gear icon in sidebar

  2. Navigate to Appearance

    • May be under "General" or separate "Appearance" category

  3. Select Theme

    Theme: [Light ▼]

Options:
• Light
• Dark
• Auto (Follow System)

  4. Apply Changes

    • Usually immediate, no restart needed
    • Interface updates instantly

Custom Accent Colors

Some versions allow you to customize the accent color (the color used for buttons, highlights, and active elements).

Available Colors:

Accent color: [SeaMeet Blue ▼]

Options typically include:
• SeaMeet Blue (default)
• Blue
• Green
• Red
• Purple
• Orange
• Pink
• Teal
• Gray

How to Set:

Settings → Appearance → Accent Color
Select your preferred color
Changes apply immediately

Why Customize:

  • Personal preference
  • Match your brand colors
  • Accessibility (some colors easier to see)
  • Visual distinction from other apps

Section 3: Regional Settings

Date Format

Different regions display dates differently. SeaMeet can match your local convention.

Available Formats:

MM/DD/YYYY (US Standard):

Date format: [MM/DD/YYYY ▼]
Examples:
• 01/15/2024 (January 15, 2024)
• 12/25/2024 (December 25, 2024)

DD/MM/YYYY (European/International):

Date format: [DD/MM/YYYY ▼]
Examples:
• 15/01/2024 (15 January 2024)
• 25/12/2024 (25 December 2024)

YYYY-MM-DD (ISO 8601 Standard):

Date format: [YYYY-MM-DD ▼]
Examples:
• 2024-01-15 (January 15, 2024)
• 2024-12-25 (December 25, 2024)

DD-MM-YYYY (Alternative):

Date format: [DD-MM-YYYY ▼]
Examples:
• 15-01-2024
• 25-12-2024

YYYY/MM/DD (Asian Format):

Date format: [YYYY/MM/DD ▼]
Examples:
• 2024/01/15
• 2024/12/25

Where Date Format Appears:

  • Recording timestamps in the library
  • File creation dates displayed in SeaMeet
  • Sorting and filtering by date
  • Export file names (if using date)

Time Format

Choose between 12-hour (AM/PM) and 24-hour formats.

12-Hour Format (AM/PM):

Time format: [12-hour ▼]
Examples:
• 2:30 PM
• 9:15 AM
• 11:45 PM

24-Hour Format:

Time format: [24-hour ▼]
Examples:
• 14:30 (2:30 PM)
• 09:15 (9:15 AM)
• 23:45 (11:45 PM)

Where Time Format Appears:

  • Recording timestamps
  • Duration displays
  • Start/stop times
  • Export metadata

Time Zone Settings

SeaMeet uses your system time zone by default, but you can configure it explicitly.

Automatic (System Time Zone):

Time zone: [Automatic (System) ▼]
Uses your computer's time zone setting

Manual Selection:

Time zone: [Manual ▼]
Select from list:
• UTC (Coordinated Universal Time)
• EST (Eastern Standard Time, UTC-5)
• CST (Central Standard Time, UTC-6)
• MST (Mountain Standard Time, UTC-7)
• PST (Pacific Standard Time, UTC-8)
• GMT (Greenwich Mean Time, UTC+0)
• CET (Central European Time, UTC+1)
• [+ hundreds more...]

Why Set Time Zone:

  • Accurate timestamps when traveling
  • Consistent times across devices
  • Correct sorting by time
  • Accurate duration calculations

Daylight Saving Time:

  • SeaMeet follows system settings
  • Automatically adjusts for DST transitions
  • No manual intervention needed

First Day of Week

Options:

First day of week: [Sunday ▼]

Options:
• Sunday (US, Canada, Japan, etc.)
• Monday (Most of Europe, Asia, Africa)
• Saturday (Some Middle Eastern countries)

Where This Appears:

  • Calendar views (if available)
  • Week-based grouping
  • Scheduling interfaces

Number Format

Different regions use different symbols for decimals and thousands separators.

US/UK Format:

Number format: [1,234.56 ▼]
• Period (.) for decimal
• Comma (,) for thousands
Example: $1,234.56

European Format:

Number format: [1.234,56 ▼]
• Comma (,) for decimal
• Period (.) for thousands
Example: €1.234,56

Where This Appears:

  • File size displays
  • Duration in minutes/seconds
  • Storage usage statistics
  • Performance metrics

Section 4: Interface Customization

Floater Appearance

The Floater (floating control widget) can be customized to match your preferences.

Floater Size:

Floater size: [Normal ▼]

Options:
• Small — 48×48 pixels, minimal
• Normal — 64×64 pixels (default)
• Large — 80×80 pixels, easier to see/click

Floater Transparency:

Floater opacity: [90% ▼]

Options:
• 100% — Fully opaque
• 90% — Slightly transparent (default)
• 75% — Moderately transparent
• 50% — Very transparent
• 25% — Almost invisible

Why Adjust Opacity:

  • Lower opacity = less intrusive
  • Can see content behind Floater
  • But harder to see/find
  • Balance based on your workflow

Floater Position:

Default position: [Bottom-right ▼]

Options:
• Top-left
• Top-right
• Bottom-left
• Bottom-right

Remember Position:

☑ Remember last position
When enabled, Floater stays where you moved it

Sidebar Customization

Sidebar Width: Some versions allow you to resize the left sidebar:

  • Drag the right edge of the sidebar
  • Make it wider to see full text
  • Make it narrower for more main panel space

Icon vs. Text Labels:

Sidebar style: [Icons + Text ▼]

Options:
• Icons + Text — Full labels (recommended)
• Icons Only — Compact, just symbols
• Text Only — No icons, just words

Icons + Text:

  • Easier to learn
  • Clear identification
  • Takes more space

Icons Only:

  • Compact
  • More main panel space
  • Requires learning icons
  • Good for experienced users

Font Settings

Some versions allow font customization:

Interface Font:

Font family: [System Default ▼]
Options:
• System Default (follows OS)
• Sans-serif (clean, modern)
• Serif (traditional, readable)
• Monospace (technical)

Font Size:

Font size: [Medium ▼]

Options:
• Small — Compact, more content visible
• Medium — Balanced (default)
• Large — Easier to read
• Extra Large — Accessibility

Adjust for:

  • Vision needs
  • Screen resolution (high DPI = larger fonts)
  • Personal preference
  • Distance from screen

Section 5: Accessibility Options

High Contrast Mode

☑ Enable high contrast mode

What It Does:

  • Maximizes contrast between elements
  • Easier to see for vision impairments
  • Helps with color blindness
  • Meets accessibility standards

Appearance:

  • Pure black and white (or high-contrast colors)
  • No subtle grays
  • Clear borders around everything
  • Bold text

Reduced Motion

☑ Reduce motion and animations

What It Does:

  • Disables animated transitions
  • Removes zoom effects
  • Stops sliding animations
  • Static, immediate changes

Benefits:

  • Helps users with motion sensitivity/vertigo
  • Faster interface response
  • Less distracting
  • Better for older computers

Screen Reader Support

SeaMeet supports screen readers for visually impaired users:

Compatible With:

  • Windows: NVDA, JAWS, Narrator
  • macOS: VoiceOver
  • Linux: Orca

Features:

  • All buttons labeled for screen readers
  • Status announcements
  • Keyboard navigation support
  • Focus indicators

Enabling:

  1. Enable your screen reader at OS level
  2. SeaMeet automatically detects it
  3. Increases verbosity for announcements

Keyboard Navigation

SeaMeet is fully keyboard accessible:

Navigation Keys:

Tab — Move to next control
Shift + Tab — Move to previous control
Space or Enter — Activate button
Arrow keys — Navigate lists/menus
Esc — Close dialog/cancel

Keyboard Shortcuts: All main functions have keyboard shortcuts:

  • See Chapter 13 for full list
  • No mouse required

Focus Indicators:

  • Clear visual highlight on focused element
  • Helps keyboard users see where they are
  • Essential for accessibility

Section 6: Best Practices

Choosing the Right Settings

For Comfort:

  • Use Dark mode at night
  • Use Light mode during day
  • Enable Auto to follow system
  • Adjust font size for your vision

For Productivity:

  • Set language you're most fluent in
  • Use date/time formats that match your region
  • Set Floater position that doesn't block your work
  • Choose icon style based on familiarity

For Accessibility:

  • Use High Contrast if needed
  • Enable Reduce Motion if sensitive
  • Set font size to Large or Extra Large
  • Use keyboard navigation

For Multi-Region Users:

  • Set time zone to your current location
  • Use international date format (YYYY-MM-DD)
  • Use 24-hour time for clarity
  • Keep language in your preferred tongue regardless of location

Settings That Travel With You

If You Move Between Countries:

  1. Keep Language: Don't change—stay in your native language
  2. Update Time Zone: Set to current location
  3. Consider Date Format: May want to adjust to local convention
  4. Time Format: 24-hour is universal—consider keeping it

If You Share Your Computer:

  1. Create Separate User Accounts:

    • Each person has their own SeaMeet settings
    • Windows/macOS user accounts are independent

  2. Or Use System Theme:

    • Set SeaMeet to "Auto (Follow System)"
    • Each user sets their OS theme
    • SeaMeet matches automatically

Troubleshooting Appearance Issues

Problem: Theme Doesn't Change

Solutions:

  1. Apply settings and wait 5 seconds
  2. Restart SeaMeet
  3. Check if using "Follow System" (change OS theme)
  4. Update graphics drivers
  5. Reinstall SeaMeet if persistent

Problem: Language Partially Translated

Solutions:

  1. Some languages may be incomplete
  2. Switch to a fully translated language
  3. Check for SeaMeet updates (translations added over time)
  4. Report missing translations to support

Problem: Text Too Small/Large

Solutions:

  1. Adjust font size in settings
  2. Use system scaling (Windows: Settings → Display → Scale)
  3. macOS: System Preferences → Displays → Resolution
  4. Some versions support Ctrl +/- to zoom interface

Problem: Floater Hard to See

Solutions:

  1. Increase Floater size (Large)
  2. Increase opacity (100%)
  3. Change position to less cluttered area
  4. Change accent color to something more visible
