Does SeaMeet AI Note Taker Support In-Person Meetings and Speaker Identification?

Answer

You can use a computer to open Google Meet for meeting recording during in-person meetings with SeaMeet AI Note Taker. However, features like changing the speaker for in-person meetings are not yet available and are expected to be provided in Q1 SeaMeet’s Speaker ID feature helps identify different speakers in recorded meetings, but manual speaker assignment for in-person meetings is coming soon.
