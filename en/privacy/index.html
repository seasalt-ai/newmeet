SeaMeet Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 2/1/2026

SeaMeet (“SeaMeet”, “we”, “our”, “us”) is a product of Seasalt.ai, Inc. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use:

  • The SeaMeet Desktop App for Windows and macOS
  • The SeaMeet Cloud Service
  • The SeaMeet.ai website

We are committed to transparency and to giving users full control over their data.

1. SeaMeet Desktop App (Windows/macOS)

SeaMeet Desktop is designed to run locally on your device.
When you record audio, video, or your screen using the desktop app:

We do NOT collect or receive:

  • Audio recordings
  • Video recordings
  • Screen content
  • Meeting metadata
  • Personal files
  • Contacts or identities

All recordings remain on your device only, unless you explicitly choose to upload them to SeaMeet Cloud.

We do NOT transmit usage data by default.

Optional: Anonymous User Experience Improvement Program

You may voluntarily opt in to share anonymous technical diagnostics to help improve SeaMeet.
If you opt in, we may collect:

  • App version
  • OS version
  • Device type
  • Performance metrics (CPU spikes, dropped frames, encoder stalls)
  • Feature usage counts
  • Crash logs and error reports

We NEVER collect through this program:

  • Audio or video content
  • Screen content
  • Meeting content
  • Personal files
  • User identities

You may opt out at any time in Settings → Privacy & Data.

2. SeaMeet Cloud Service

SeaMeet Cloud is an optional service that allows users to record meetings, store audio, and generate transcripts or summaries.

When you use SeaMeet Cloud, we may collect:

A. Meeting Content (only when you choose to upload or record via cloud):

  • Audio recordings
  • Transcriptions
  • Meeting titles, timestamps, and metadata

B. Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Organization (if applicable)
  • Authentication credentials (hashed)

C. Usage Information

  • Cloud recording activity
  • Device/browser type
  • IP address (for security and abuse prevention)

D. Third‑Party Integrations (optional)

If you connect SeaMeet Cloud to services such as Google Drive or calendar systems, we may process:

  • File export data
  • Calendar event metadata
  • OAuth tokens (securely stored and revocable at any time)

We adhere to all applicable platform policies, including Google API Services User Data Policy.

3. How We Use the Information

We use collected data to:

  • Provide and maintain SeaMeet Cloud services
  • Generate transcripts, summaries, and meeting insights
  • Where AI-powered features are used, transcript text may be shared with third-party AI services as described in Section 8, subject to your consent.
  • Improve product performance and reliability
  • Respond to support requests
  • Secure our systems and prevent abuse

We do not sell personal data.

4. How We Protect Your Information

We use industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encrypted transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
  • Encrypted storage for cloud recordings
  • Access controls and audit logs
  • Limited employee access on a need-to-know basis

Recordings stored in SeaMeet Cloud are encrypted at rest.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Desktop recordings remain on your device until you delete them.
  • Cloud recordings are retained until you delete them or your account is closed.
  • You may request deletion of all cloud data by emailing info@seasalt.ai.
  • We process valid deletion requests within a reasonable timeframe.

6. Cookies & Website Analytics

SeaMeet.ai may use cookies for basic site functionality and aggregated analytics.
You may disable cookies in your browser settings.

7. Children’s Privacy

SeaMeet is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Third‑Party Services and AI Processing

SeaMeet Cloud uses third-party providers for cloud storage, authentication, and analytics (anonymous and aggregated only).

8.1 AI‑Powered Features

To provide meeting summarization, action item extraction, and translation, SeaMeet sends meeting transcript text to Google LLC and OpenAI. We do not send audio recordings, account credentials, or any other personally identifiable information.

These providers act as data processors on our behalf and do not use your data to train their AI models.

Before your transcripts are processed, you will be asked for explicit consent in the app. You may withdraw consent or disable AI features anytime in Settings → AI Features; core recording functionality remains available regardless.

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • OpenAI Privacy Policy

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Updates will be posted on SeaMeet.ai with a revised “Last Updated” date.

10. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact:
📧 info@seameet.ai
